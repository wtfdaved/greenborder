Visiting From El Paso? Know Before You Go

Quick answers about buying legal cannabis across the border in New Mexico.

Is cannabis legal near El Paso, Texas?
Cannabis is legal for adults 21+ in New Mexico, including Sunland Park, Santa Teresa, Chaparral, and Anthony — all within a short drive of El Paso. It remains illegal in Texas and federally, so it cannot be transported across the state line.
Can Texas residents buy cannabis in New Mexico?
Yes. New Mexico dispensaries sell to any adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID, regardless of home state. Purchases must be consumed in New Mexico — bringing cannabis back into Texas is a crime.
What do I need to buy from a dispensary?
A valid government-issued photo ID proving you are 21 or older. Most dispensaries accept cash and debit; credit cards are often unavailable due to federal banking rules.
Which dispensary is closest to El Paso?
Dispensaries in Sunland Park, NM are the closest to El Paso — roughly 10–15 minutes from the west side and downtown. Chaparral serves Northeast El Paso, while Santa Teresa and Anthony are convenient from I-10 and the Upper Valley.
Can I take my purchase back to Texas?
No. Possessing cannabis in Texas remains illegal, and crossing the state line with it can carry serious penalties. Several dispensaries offer on-site consumption lounges where products can be enjoyed legally in New Mexico.
Are there consumption lounges near El Paso?
Yes. Several dispensaries in Sunland Park and Chaparral operate licensed consumption lounges where adults 21+ can legally consume on site — some with food service and events. Use the 🛋️ Lounge filter above to find them.
